For the judges / two-minute walkthrough

One question. One intervention.
A visible change in direction.

CureLab explores how a researcher can inspect and redirect a pair of independent AI scientists. The strongest demonstration is the connection between human input, delivered peer messages, and the next research decision.

  1. 0:00–0:20 · The question

    Show a specific task and the two independent scientists. Explain that experiments are synthetic.

  2. 0:20–0:55 · The exchange

    Open the active discussion and play both voices. Expand a Relay delivery receipt and an agent’s reading desk.

  3. 0:55–1:25 · The intervention

    Record “prioritize viability controls,” review and submit. Show the new guidance and the scientists’ subsequent replies.

  4. 1:25–2:00 · The evidence

    Inspect an approved, completed simulation, its charts and Scientific PR. Close with the remaining evidence gap.

How this maps to the judging criteria

Real-time AI & track technology: human input changes subsequent model context, with actual Agent Relay delivery. Originality: independent research, peer challenge and preserved dissent. Execution: show working exchanges and a completed simulation. UX: visible speaker, human control and inspectable workspaces. Impact: make exploratory research easier to question and audit; clinical benefit is not yet validated.

Per the supplied judging slide: the first round reviews the demo video and project description; eight finalists advance to a two-minute live demo on their own laptops. Each of the five criteria is scored from 1 to 5.

Presenter preparation: use a real session already at the discussion stage, and clearly identify any previously completed run. Provider response times vary; never present prerecorded responses as live.

Inside the laboratory

Follow the science.
Question every step.

From a first hypothesis to a reviewable research artifact, the workspace keeps the human, the evidence and the disagreements visible.

01 / RESEARCH TEAM

Two perspectives, their own workspaces.

OpenAI and Groq research independently in separate Freestyle VMs before seeing each other’s reports. Expand each scientist’s workspace to inspect jobs, activity logs, reports and the papers supplied to the model. Shared critique and discussion stay in the main room.

02 / HUMAN DIRECTION

Your research desk, every session.

Persistent human chat lets you set a specific task, ask questions and redirect the discussion. Experiments still require an explicit protocol approval.

03 / CELL OBSERVATORY

A spatial view of the cells.

Explore a procedural 3D islet, inspect alpha, beta and delta cells, switch to a single cell, and view conceptual signaling. The model is illustrative, not a biological digital twin.

04 / EXPERIMENTS

Approve. Simulate. Inspect.

Review an immutable protocol, run a seeded synthetic assay, inspect controls and QC, and compare earlier experiment versions without overwriting their results.

05 / RESULTS

More than one view of a result.

Time courses, group means, well distributions, secretion–viability scatter and full-well heatmaps. Inspect exact values in accessible tables; export SVG charts and CSV measurements.

06 / EVIDENCE

Know what they actually read.

Europe PMC abstract retrieval, source links and content hashes make supplied evidence inspectable. Suggested GEO and ChEMBL sources are distinguished from data actually analyzed.

07 / RESEARCH RECORD

Keep the disagreement. Keep the trail.

A Scientific PR gathers both reviews and preserves dissent. SQLite mission snapshots, queued jobs, audit events, artifact hashes and JSON research exports keep the workflow inspectable and support recovery.

The research loop

A question becomes a record.

  1. Assign a precise task

    The human defines the question, constraints and desired experiment.

  2. Research independently

    Each scientist develops a first report in its own VM.

  3. Exchange and challenge

    Relay delivers peer context for critiques and discussion.

  4. Approve the protocol

    The human reviews the exact proposed synthetic assay.

  5. Run the simulation

    A seeded runner produces wells, outcomes and QC.

  6. Explore the results

    Charts, versions and exact data stay available for inspection.

  7. Review independently

    Both scientists assess the findings and limitations.

  8. Publish a research artifact

    The Scientific PR records evidence, uncertainty and dissent.

Why is the room sometimes quiet?

The scientists may still be researching independently, generating their next reply, or waiting for your input. A successful voice test checks output; it does not create a discussion. Voice needs live mode and configured server credentials.

Is this a live audio call between agents?

This implementation speaks committed text turns. It uses buffered audio and click-to-interrupt recording, not a full-duplex voice call. Agent Relay carries text messages; audio is generated separately.
